About NetCheck

        
Local diagnostics for your network connection and browser security.

      


      

        
        

          

            
            Dashboard
          

          

            
Your network at a glance. Shows an overall score, your IP address and location, recent download speed, and latency — all updated in real time as tests complete.

          

        


        
        

          

            
            DNS Check
          

          

            
Detects your IP address, DNS resolver configuration, and checks DNS security features such as DNSSEC validation, DoH encryption, and malware filtering. Includes a DNS lookup tool to query domains for any record type and WebRTC leak detection.

          

        


        
        

          

            
            Speed Test
          

          

            
Measures download and upload speed, latency, jitter, and bufferbloat using your nearest Cloudflare edge server. Displays real-time performance graphs and a 5-factor quality grade.

          

        


        
        

          

            
            TLS Inspector
          

          

            
Inspects the TLS connection to Cloudflare's edge, reporting protocol version, cipher suite, key exchange, forward secrecy support, and certificate details. Assigns an A+–F security grade.

          

        


        
        

          

            
            Ad Block Test
          

          

            
Checks how well your ad blocker or browser is blocking ad scripts, trackers, analytics, social media widgets, and fingerprinting scripts. Gives a score from 0 to 100 with category-specific recommendations.

          

        


        
        

          

            
            Headers
          

          

            
Scans any website for 10 critical security headers: HSTS, CSP, X-Frame-Options, Referrer-Policy, and more. Provides a letter grade, detailed per-header analysis, and suggests improvements.

          

        


        
        

          

            
            Fingerprint
          

          

            
Analyzes how unique your browser fingerprint is across 6 categories: Canvas, WebGL, Audio, Fonts, Screen, and Navigator properties. Provides tailored privacy protection tips to reduce your online trackability.

          

        


        
        

          

            
            Connection Quality
          

          

            
Inspects your browser connection info (type, downlink, RTT), TLS details, request timing breakdown (DNS / TCP / TLS / TTFB / Download), and runs a stability test measuring jitter and packet loss over 30 pings. Computes an overall A+–F grade.

          

        


        
        

          

            
            Network Map
          

          

            
Measures your latency to global network endpoints, giving a regional view of connection health. Visualizes closest regions, quality ratings, and server relay latency via Cloudflare’s distributed edge.

          

        


        
        

          

            
            History
          

          

            
Tracks your speed test results over time with a visual bar chart, average stats and trend indicator. Filter by 7 days, 30 days, or view all history. Compare any two tests side by side and export data as CSV.

          

        


        
        

          

            
            AI Analysis
          

          

            
Get AI-powered insights into your network health. Cloud AI (default) sends anonymized test metrics to Cloudflare Workers AI for instant analysis — no data is stored or logged. On-Device AI runs a local language model entirely in your browser for complete privacy.

          

        


        
        

          

            
            Share & Export
          

          

            
Copy a text summary of any test result to your clipboard via the share button, or export a full report as Markdown or PDF. Supports 6 languages with automatic localization.

          

        


        
        

          

            
            Email Security
          

          

            
Checks a domain's SPF, DKIM, and DMARC DNS records to verify email authentication is properly configured. Provides an A+–F security grade with specific steps to fix missing or misconfigured records.

          

        


        
        

          

            
            HTTP/3 Test
          

          

            
Tests whether your connection supports HTTP/3 (QUIC), the next-generation web protocol. Runs 5 sequential pings to detect protocol negotiation, measures latency, and checks for 0-RTT connection support and Alt-Svc advertisement.

          

        


        
        

          

            
            Cookie Audit
          

          

            
Analyzes first-party cookies set by the current site, classifying each as essential, analytics, advertising, or unknown. Reports total count, storage size, security prefix usage, and flags tracking cookies with recommendations to reduce your footprint.

          

        


        
        

          

            
            Breach Check
          

          

            
Check if a password has appeared in known data breaches using Have I Been Pwned's k-anonymity API. Your password never leaves your browser — only the first 5 characters of its SHA-1 hash are sent. Provides a risk assessment with severity levels.

          

        


        
        

          

            
            CSP Deep Analyzer
          

          

            
Parses Content-Security-Policy directives and flags misconfigurations like unsafe-inline, unsafe-eval, wildcard sources, and missing directives. Provides a sub-grade with severity-ranked findings and remediation suggestions.

          

        


        
        

          

            
            BIMI & MTA-STS
          

          

            
Checks BIMI records for brand logo display in email clients, and MTA-STS policies for SMTP TLS enforcement. Extends email security beyond SPF/DKIM/DMARC with next-generation standards.

          

        

      


      

        

          

            
            
Everything runs locally in your browser.
No data is collected, stored, or sent anywhere.
