Feature 4: Test Comparison

Side-by-side comparison of any two test runs. Select from history timeline, see diff of all metrics highlighted in green (improve) / red (regressed).

Enables answering questions like: "Did switching DNS providers improve my latency?" or "Is my connection worse at night?"
